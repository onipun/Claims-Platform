## Known gaps

- Nothing here comes from watching a claims handler work, and no practitioner has reviewed it.
- Rules come from individual markets. Other markets have no late-report rule, and use the Malaysian document list as a labelled placeholder.
- The six markets are an assumption (SG, HK, MY, TH, ID, AU). The brief doesn't name them.
- Property claim types aren't sourced from Chubb: its property form refused download. They're a working list.
- Service levels and authority limits are placeholders.
